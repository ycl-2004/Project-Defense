# Surface brief: the session workspace (/interview), and the world the other routes inherit

Scope: /interview is the surface that proves the world; /, /setup and /report inherit it. Visitor mode: Operate for /interview, /setup, /report; Persuade for /. Audience: students 13–18 practising before a mentor check-in, on a laptop; mentors reading reports. Feel: one-on-one with a serious, kind mentor. Refuse: AI-template softness and cold corporate SaaS.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every question is pinned to the student's own lines the way a teacher's red pen marks an exercise book, set like a reference edition: code and conversation as twin columns around a centre rail of keys. Refuses the AI-chat default (bubbles beside an editor) and the cream study-desk look.

OWN-WORLD: Cool paper white, iron ink, one red pen for every mentor mark, ballpoint blue for everything the student writes, pale rule-blue hairlines. 1px rules, square detents, no radius, no shadows. Song for printed questions, Kai for the mentor's hand, sans for UI, Source Serif numerals and small-caps apparatus, JetBrains Mono for code.

STORY: The student sees exactly which lines the mentor is pointing at, answers in their own blue words, and watches the red marks follow; they leave knowing what they explained. The mentor later reads the same marks in the report.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top rule bar: wordmark left, five square detents centre (done filled ink, current filled red, pending hairline), leave, EN and theme right. Left column code with gutter numbers and a red bracket on the cited range; a 56px centre rail carrying the question key at its first line, detents at both ends, keys for lines the question names, and the mentor's short margin note in red Kai beside its line; right column: meta line, the question in Song, the student's answer in blue, the follow-up in red Kai, a ruled composer with hint, skip and send.
